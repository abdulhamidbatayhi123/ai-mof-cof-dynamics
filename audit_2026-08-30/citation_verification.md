## 2. CORRECTIONS REQUIRED

**C1 — Reiss *et al.* sPOD: fabricated author.**
Believed: "Reiss, Schulze, Sesterhenn & Noack."
Source: fourth author is **Volker Mehrmann**. Noack is not an author.
Corrected: "J. Reiss, P. Schulze, J. Sesterhenn and V. Mehrmann, *SIAM J. Sci. Comput.* **40**(3), A1322–A1344 (2018)."

**C2 — Two-wave warp novelty: FALSIFIED.**
Believed: a two-parameter warp aligning two distinct waves independently is novel or unclaimed.
Source: this is the *founding worked example* of sPOD — a pressure pulse splitting into c⁺=+1 and c⁻=−1 waves, reconstructed with two modes (one per co-moving frame) versus 80+ POD modes; the general formulation is written for arbitrary Nₛ independent transports, q(xᵢ,tₙ) ≈ Σₖ T^{c_k}(Σ_l α_l^k(tₙ)φ_l^k(xᵢ)). Multi-front alignment is further covered by Nair & Balajewicz (2019), Mendible *et al.* (2020), Zorawski *et al.* (2024), Krah *et al.* (2025), Zucatti & Zahr (2025).
Corrected: "The two-wave warp is an instance of the transformation-based, co-moving-frame family; the two-transport case is the canonical sPOD example (Reiss et al., 2018), and multi-front variants are established (Nair & Balajewicz, 2019; Mendible et al., 2020; Krah et al., 2025). Our contribution is [narrow claim: specific parametrisation / estimation procedure / MOF-COF application], not multi-front alignment itself." Every sentence implying the warp is new must be deleted.

**C3 — Kiyani *et al.*: the premise inverts the source.**
Believed: Kiyani reports PIKANs an order of magnitude more accurate than PINNs (counter-evidence to L3).
Source: the "orders-of-magnitude accuracy improvements" are attributed to the **optimizer** (SSBFGS/SSBroyden self-scaled quasi-Newton vs BFGS/L-BFGS), with PINNs and PIKANs both as test beds. On architecture the abstract says PIKANs are "effective and comparable in accuracy with PINNs."
Corrected: "Kiyani et al. (2025) attribute their orders-of-magnitude gains to self-scaled quasi-Newton optimizers rather than to architecture, and describe PIKANs as 'comparable in accuracy with PINNs' — a second Karniadakis-group endorsement of parity." Cite as **support** for L3, not as counter-evidence.

**C4 — Rigas *et al.* 2026: unqualified L3 claim is falsified.**
Believed: no matched-parameter KAN-beats-MLP PDE result exists to pre-empt.
Source: peer-reviewed CMAME, nine forward PDE benchmarks, fixed pipeline, explicitly parameter-matched, RGA KANs beat cPIKANs *and* MLP-based PirateNets "often by several orders of magnitude."
Corrected: "Vanilla or lightly-tuned KANs at matched parameter budgets do not outperform MLPs and are less robust; closing the gap requires architecture- and initialisation-specific engineering — residual gating, adaptive components and a bespoke variance-preserving initialisation (Rigas et al., CMAME 452:118761, 2026)." Their own baseline supports L3: plain cPIKANs stagnate in the diffusion phase and diverge where RGA KANs are stable.

### Classical / numerical ground truth
- **LDF coefficient:** k = **15** D/R² (Glueckauf 1955). Perry's Sec. 16 forms: k_n = 15(1−ε)ρ_p D_pi/r_p²; k_n = 15 ε_s D_si/r_s²; combined Eq. (16-84) k_n^c = 15 ε_s D_si/r_p² + 15(1−ε)ρ_p D_pi/r_p²; dimensionless N = 15 D_s L/(v_ref R_p²). Regime caveat: 15 is the near-equilibrium / long-cycle value; effective coefficient rises toward ~40 at short cycle times (Sircar & Hufton, *Adsorption* 6:137, 2000).
- **Linear-isotherm packed-bed solution (L0):** c*ᵢ = J(Nξ, Nτ₁), n*ᵢ = 1 − J(Nτ₁, Nξ) — Perry's Eq. (16-147); J function per Hiester & Vermeulen (1952), Eq. (16-148). Valid for R = 1, LDF/film/reaction kinetics, **no axial dispersion** (Anzelius 1926; Schumann 1929).
- **Klinkenberg approximation (L7 control):** C/C_F ≈ ½[1 + erf(√τ − √ξ + 1/(8√τ) + 1/(8√ξ))], τ = K(t − z/u_i), ξ = (K H z/u_i)((1−ε_b)/ε_b); stated accuracy <0.6% for ξ > 2.0 — **secondary, unverified** (Klinkenberg 1954).
- **Dirichlet (first-type) inlet, semi-infinite CDE — Ogata & Banks Eq. 13:** C/C₀ = ½[erfc((x−ut)/(2√(Dt))) + exp(ux/D)·erfc((x+ut)/(2√(Dt)))].
- **Flux (third-type) inlet, semi-infinite CDE — van Genuchten & Alves Case A2:** A₀(x,t) = ½ erfc[(Rx−vt)/(2(DRt)^½)] + (v²t/(πDR))^½ exp[−(Rx−vt)²/(4DRt)] − ½(1 + vx/D + v²t/(DR)) exp(vx/D) erfc[(Rx+vt)/(2(DRt)^½)]. Third-type BC: −D ∂c/∂x + vc = v g(t) at x = 0 (their Eq. 9b). At Péclet 5 the first-type profiles lie "much higher" than third-type throughout the profile; the gap shrinks by Pe = 20 (their p. 46).
- **Thermodynamic correction factor (Lassitter Eq. 2):** D_t(c) = D₀(c)(∂ln f/∂ln c)_T; corrected diffusivity = Maxwell–Stefan diffusivity for single components.
- **Combined-wave criterion (Perry's Sec. 16, Fig. 16-19, "After Tudge"):** shock chord taken wherever the path slope in the isotherm plane would decrease; shock speed = Δflux / Δaccumulated quantity. Types II, IV, V have one or more inflection points.
