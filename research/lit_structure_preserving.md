## Source log (appended as fetched)

### A. Conservative baselines (HNN / LNN)

- **S1 FETCHED** arXiv:1906.01563 (https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.01563). "Hamiltonian Neural Networks". Sam Greydanus, Misko Dzamba, Jason Yosinski (2019).
  Supports: the architecture is built so the learned dynamics conserve a learned energy exactly; shown on the two-body problem and a pixel pendulum; time-reversibility is advertised as a feature. Quote: "learn and respect exact conservation laws". Relevance: a reversible, energy-conserving flow is the WRONG prior for an adsorption column, whose dynamics are irreversible (dispersion, LDF relaxation, heat loss).
- **S2 FETCHED** arXiv:2003.04630 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.04630). "Lagrangian Neural Networks". Miles Cranmer, Sam Greydanus, Stephan Hoyer, Peter Battaglia, David Spergel, Shirley Ho (2020).
  Supports: parameterises an arbitrary Lagrangian; demos double pendulum, relativistic particle, 1D wave equation (graph variant). The abstract sells energy conservation where a baseline "incurs dissipation" — i.e. dissipation is treated as an error to remove. Relevance: same mismatch as S1; there is no Lagrangian (second-order, time-reversible) structure in a first-order parabolic advection-dispersion-LDF-heat system.

### B. Dissipative extensions: port-Hamiltonian / dissipative Hamiltonian

- **S3 FETCHED** arXiv:2107.08024 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.08024). "Port-Hamiltonian Neural Networks for Learning Explicit Time-Dependent Dynamical Systems". Shaan Desai, Marios Mattheakis, David Sondak, Pavlos Protopapas, Stephen Roberts (2021), Phys. Rev. E 104, 034312 (venue as shown on arXiv page).
  Supports: port-Hamiltonian embedding captures dissipation and time-dependent forcing; recovers Hamiltonian, forcing and damping coefficients; demos are low-dimensional mechanical oscillators incl. chaotic Duffing.
- **S4 FETCHED** arXiv:2002.08860 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.08860). "Dissipative SymODEN: Encoding Hamiltonian Dynamics with Dissipation and Control into Deep Learning". Yaofeng Desmond Zhong, Biswadip Dey, Amit Chakraborty (2020, ICLR 2020 workshop).
  Supports: encodes port-Hamiltonian dynamics with dissipation and external input; learns inertia/dissipation/potential; intended for energy-based control. Abstract does not list systems (paper body not opened; the systems are mechanical per the framing — not verified).
- **S5 FETCHED** arXiv:2201.10085 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.10085). "Dissipative Hamiltonian Neural Networks: Learning Dissipative and Conservative Dynamics Separately". Andrew Sosanya, Sam Greydanus (2022).
  Supports: learns a Hamiltonian plus a Rayleigh dissipation function (implicit Helmholtz decomposition); demos damped mass-spring, unseen friction coefficients, ocean-current data. Relevance: still a mechanical (q,p) picture with friction bolted on; the adsorption state (c, q, T) is not a canonical pair.

### C. GENERIC / metriplectic / Onsager (the established route for thermodynamic systems)

- **S6 FETCHED** arXiv:2004.04653 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.04653). "Structure-preserving neural networks". Quercus Hernández, Alberto Badias, David Gonzalez, Francisco Chinesta, Elias Cueto (2020 on arXiv; journal version not opened).
  Supports: enforces the metriplectic GENERIC structure (Grmela & Oettinger 1997) so predictions conserve energy and dissipate/produce entropy consistently with the second law; conservative and dissipative, discrete and continuum examples. Guarantee is architectural (via the bracket structure), not a penalty.
- **S7 FETCHED** arXiv:2109.00092 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.00092). "GFINNs: GENERIC Formalism Informed Neural Networks for Deterministic and Stochastic Dynamical Systems". Zhen Zhang, Yeonjong Shin, George Em Karniadakis (2021).
  Supports: satisfies the GENERIC degeneracy conditions by construction; universal-approximation result; demos: two gas containers exchanging heat and volume, thermoelastic double pendulum, Langevin dynamics. Note: "gas containers exchanging heat" is the closest toy analogue to a thermal-exchange system, but it is a lumped 0-D ODE, not a PDE column.
- **S8 FETCHED** arXiv:2203.01874 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.01874). "Thermodynamics-informed graph neural networks". Quercus Hernández, Alberto Badías, Francisco Chinesta, Elías Cueto (2022; IEEE TAI per arXiv page).
  Supports: GNN + GENERIC for dissipative systems in Eulerian (fluid) and Lagrangian (solid) descriptions; <3% relative mean errors reported. Relevance: shows GENERIC can be carried to mesh/graph-discretised continua — the path a column (1-D graph of cells) would take.
- **S9 FETCHED** arXiv:2106.12619 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.12619). "Machine learning structure preserving brackets for forecasting irreversible processes". Kookjin Lee, Nathaniel A. Trask, Panos Stinis (2021).
  Supports: parameterises metriplectic brackets so learned energy is conserved and learned entropy is non-decreasing; preserves fluctuation-dissipation with thermal noise; reports better generalisation than black-box or penalty-based approaches.
- **S10 FETCHED** arXiv:2009.02327 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.02327). "OnsagerNet: Learning Stable and Interpretable Dynamics using a Generalized Onsager Principle". Haijun Yu, Xinyuan Tian, Weinan E, Qianxiao Li (2020; Phys. Rev. Fluids 6, 114402 per arXiv page).
  Supports: learned ODEs in generalised-Onsager form (energy, dissipation matrix, conservative part) for stability/interpretability; demos: benchmark ODEs and Rayleigh-Bénard convection reduced to Lorenz-like low-dimensional models via an autoencoder. Relevance: this is the natural template for a reduced-order column model (autoencoder latent + Onsager dynamics) — nothing in the abstract about sorption.

- **S11 FETCHED** arXiv:2007.03758 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.03758). "Deep learning of thermodynamics-aware reduced-order models from data". Quercus Hernandez, Alberto Badias, David Gonzalez, Francisco Chinesta, Elias Cueto (2020; revised 2021).
  Supports: sparse autoencoder compresses a high-dimensional state; a GENERIC/metriplectic network evolves the latent state so that total energy is conserved and the entropy inequality holds; fluid and solid mechanics demos. Relevance: the exact recipe one would port to a discretised column (autoencoder over c(z), q(z), T(z) + GENERIC latent dynamics). Note the guarantee is on the LATENT energy/entropy, not on the decoded physical fields.
- **S12 FETCHED** arXiv:2405.16305 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.16305). "Efficiently Parameterized Neural Metriplectic Systems". Anthony Gruber, Kookjin Lee, Haksoo Lim, Noseong Park, Nathaniel Trask (2024; rev. 2025; ICLR 2025 per search snippet, SEEN-ONLY for venue).
  Supports: metriplectic parameterisation scaling quadratically in state size and rank; provably energy-conserving and entropy-stable; works when entropic variables are unknown; error estimates suggest generalisation to unseen timescales when approximation error is small.
- **S13 FETCHED** arXiv:2005.12183 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.12183). "Thermodynamics-based Artificial Neural Networks for constitutive modeling". Filippo Masi, Ioannis Stefanou, Paolo Vannucci, Victor Maffi-Berthier (2020; J. Mech. Phys. Solids 147, 104277 (2021) per arXiv page).
  Supports: free energy and dissipation rate are network outputs and stresses/internal-variable evolution are obtained by automatic differentiation, so first and second laws hold by construction; material-point elasto-plasticity. Relevance: the TANN pattern (learn a potential, differentiate it) is the template for a thermodynamically consistent LEARNED ISOTHERM (learn a grand potential / spreading pressure, differentiate for loading and isosteric heat) — see section E.
- **S14 FETCHED** arXiv:2508.06981 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.06981). "Structure-Preserving Digital Twins via Conditional Neural Whitney Forms". Brooks Kinch, Benjamin Shaffer, Elizabeth Armstrong, Michael Meehan, John Hewson, Nathaniel Trask (2025).
  Supports: learns a reduced finite-element basis plus a nonlinear conservation law in a finite-element-exterior-calculus frame; claims exact preservation of conserved quantities "regardless of data sparsity or optimization error"; benchmarks include advection-diffusion, shock hydrodynamics, electrostatics, battery thermal runaway; conditioning on parametric variables for calibration. Relevance: closest structure-preserving SURROGATE of a transport PDE with heat-release coupling (thermal runaway) found; it guarantees conservation, the abstract does not claim a second-law guarantee.

### E. Sorption-specific ML with thermodynamic constraints

- **S17 FETCHED** arXiv:2603.28328 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.28328). - **S18 FETCHED** arXiv:2604.13992 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.13992).   Supports: transfer of an H2-sorption PINN to CH4 on coals (R^2 = 0.932); reports that deep ensembles degrade because shared physics constraints narrow the admissible solution set ("ensemble collapse"); MC dropout preferred for UQ. Not applied to adsorption in the fetched abstract.
- **S20 FETCHED** arXiv:2306.07937 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.07937). "Gibbs-Duhem-Informed Neural Networks for Binary Activity Coefficient Prediction". Jan G. Rittig, Kobi C. Felton, Alexei A. Lapkin, Alexander Mitsos (2023).

### G. Continuous-time sequence models (LTC, CfC, neural ODE/CDE) and their process-control evidence

  Supports: linear first-order neurons whose time constants are modulated by nonlinear gates; theorems bound the time constant and the hidden state. ### H. Operator learning (PI-DeepONet, DeepOKAN, KAN/PIKAN) - evidence on transport and generalisation

- **S36 FETCHED** arXiv:2103.10974 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.10974). "Learning the solution operator of parametric partial differential equations with physics-informed DeepOnets". Sifan Wang, Hanwen Wang, Paris Perdikaris (2021).
  Supports: PDE residual as a soft penalty lets DeepONet train without paired input-output data (only IC/BC); predicts O(10^3) time-dependent PDE solutions in a fraction of a second, up to 1000x faster than a solver. Guarantee is soft: the physics is a penalty, nothing is conserved exactly.

### I. Dissipation-preserving operators / PDE learners and process-side hard constraints (added after first pass)

- **S46 FETCHED** arXiv:2306.06281 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.06281). "Energy-Dissipative Evolutionary Deep Operator Neural Networks". Jiahao Zhang, Shiheng Zhang, Jie Shen, Guang Lin (2023; J. Comput. Phys. per search snippet, SEEN-ONLY for venue).
  Supports: DeepONet-type operator whose parameters are evolved with the scalar-auxiliary-variable (SAV) scheme so that a MODIFIED energy dissipates unconditionally at the discrete level; heat, parametric heat and Allen-Cahn equations. Relevance: proof-of-concept that an operator learner can carry a discrete dissipation law — but only for gradient flows with a known energy functional; an advection-dominated, open (inflow/outflow) column is not a gradient flow.
- **S47 FETCHED** arXiv:2304.14374 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.14374). "Pseudo-Hamiltonian neural networks for learning partial differential equations". Sølve Eidnes, Kjetil Olsen Lye (2023; rev. 2024).

## Thermodynamic consistency constraints a learned adsorption surrogate can violate

Items marked (T) are standard textbook thermodynamics stated by this agent, NOT carried by a fetched source here; the others cite the source that supports them.

| # | Constraint | Where it lives in the column model | How a surrogate breaks it | Enforceable by construction? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | Isotherm non-decreasing in pressure / chemical potential at fixed T (for a rigid adsorbent, d<N>/d(mu) is proportional to Var(N) >= 0) (T) | q*(c,T) | free-form regressor, penalty-only PINN: S17 still had 1.4% non-monotone predictions | yes: monotone networks (non-negative weights + monotone activations), or derive q* from a learned convex potential |
| C2 | Henry's-law limit: finite, positive slope as p -> 0 (T) | q* at the column's leading edge (low c) | Freundlich/Sips-like shapes, unconstrained NNs | yes: factor q* = p * g(p,T) with g > 0 finite |

## FIT MATRIX

The physics being matched: a 1-D, open (inflow/outflow/wall), first-order, advection-dominated, dispersive, NON-ISOTHERMAL system with state (c, q, T) over z; irreversible everywhere (dispersion, LDF relaxation, heat conduction, wall loss); self-sharpening fronts for favourable isotherms, two-wave (mass + thermal) structure; control inputs at the boundary (feed, heater, valves).

The column has no canonical pairs and no conserved energy (it is an open, dissipative system). | Exact conservation of a learned energy — which is the WRONG invariant here. | Cannot represent relaxation to equilibrium, breakthrough, or heat loss; it would have to fight its own architecture. | None. |

## Gaps (each tied to what was and was not found)

1. **No structure-preserving (GENERIC, metriplectic, port-Hamiltonian, IPHS, Onsager) learned model of adsorption, sorption, chromatography or coupled heat-and-mass transfer in porous media was found.** Searches run: port-Hamiltonian + adsorption/chromatography/fixed bed; thermodynamics-informed/GENERIC + sorption/porous heat-and-mass; GENERIC + adsorption kinetics/LDF; second law/entropy production + NN surrogate + PSA/TSA/breakthrough; chromatography + structure-preserving/hard-constraint surrogate. Everything thermodynamics-structured was on mechanics, fluids, lumped gas containers, viscoelasticity, diffusion, protein phase change (S6-S12, S48, S50). Everything sorption-specific was black-box or penalty-based (S17, S18, S27, S28, S51, S53).
   - Therefore the claim **"a thermodynamically consistent (GENERIC / port-Hamiltonian) learned surrogate of non-isothermal adsorption that provably respects the second law"** appears NEW as of 2026-09-25 within the sources searched. Caveats that must travel with the claim: (a) ScienceDirect/T&F/ACS pages were blocked, so the control-theory IPHS literature (Ramirez, Maschke, Le Gorrec and co-workers) may contain a non-ML port-Hamiltonian model of an adsorption column that was not surfaced; (b) "provably respects the second law" is only meaningful if the entropy functional is the physical one (from the isotherm/heat-capacity thermodynamics), not a learned surrogate entropy (S12); (c) the novelty is the application + the open-boundary, advection-dominated, two-wave regime, not the GENERIC machinery itself, which is established (S6-S12, S50).
2. **Non-isothermal neural surrogates of breakthrough are NOT new** (S27: 1.1M-simulation binary non-isothermal surrogate, 2026; S51: non-isothermal chromatography KPI surrogate, 2026). A paper must not claim "first neural surrogate of non-isothermal adsorption".
3. **Operator learning on cyclic adsorption is isothermal so far** (S53 states the isothermal assumption explicitly). A non-isothermal operator surrogate with a thermal front is an open slot — narrower than gap 1, but real.
4. **Hard-constrained, thermodynamically consistent learned isotherms / isosteric heats / mixture (IAST) models were not found.** VLE has them (S19 hard Gibbs-Duhem; S55 HANNA); sorption has only loss penalties (S17: 98.6% monotonicity, i.e. violations remain). A learned adsorbed-phase potential that yields q*(p,T) and dH_ads by differentiation (C1-C4) is a small, clean, publishable gap — and the cheapest way for this project to say "thermodynamically consistent" honestly.
5. **Sign-correct (entropy-producing) learned uptake kinetics** inside a hybrid column model (C5) — hybrid sorption-kinetics learning exists (S28) without the constraint. Easy to add; not found done.
6. **Evidence gap for liquid networks on physical processes.** Beyond one MuJoCo benchmark (S31), no fetched evidence of LTC/CfC on chemical/thermal process modelling or control; NODE-MPC is the established precedent (S34).

## Risks

- **R1 Guarantee without accuracy.** Structure buys admissibility (no negative entropy production, no energy drift), not necessarily lower error. The project's own L4b finding is that physics-weighted training is worse or tied with data-only; a GENERIC surrogate could likewise be admissible and less accurate. The value proposition must be measured on what structure is supposed to buy: long multi-cycle rollouts, out-of-envelope stability, data efficiency (S9, S52 claims), and absence of unphysical outputs — each needs a pre-registered test.
- **R2 Whose entropy?** If S is learned (S12-style), "provably respects the second law" is a statement about a surrogate quantity. To be defensible, fix E and S from the adsorbent + gas thermodynamics (isotherm-derived adsorbed-phase entropy, heat capacities) and learn only the brackets/friction matrices.
- **R3 Open-boundary advection.** GENERIC/Onsager demos are closed or weakly open; the column is advection-dominated with inflow/outflow. Port-metriplectic (S50) and boundary-controlled IPHS (S16) are the relevant extensions but have no ML demo on such a system — implementation risk is high.
- **R4 Fronts.** Structure preservation does not solve front representation. Linear-reconstruction operators have a proven barrier for discontinuities (S37); the project measured an algebraic n-width and found the n-width bound "turned out not to matter" at the accuracy reached (RESULTS.md) — so the binding issue is optimisation/representation, not thermodynamics. A GENERIC latent model on POD/autoencoder coordinates (S11) inherits the representation problem.
- **R5 Training cost and fragility.** Degeneracy-constrained parameterisations scale poorly without S12's tricks; KAN variants are seed-fragile (S42) and collapsed in the project (10/45 DeepOKAN runs). Machine constraints (no GPU, ~1.5 GB free RAM per project memory) make large metriplectic GNNs impractical; a reduced/latent or hybrid formulation is the only feasible route.
- **R6 Over-confident UQ under constraints.** Shared physics constraints can collapse ensemble diversity (S18); if the project uses ensembles for uncertainty, hard constraints may shrink spread without reducing error.
- **R7 Hype mismatch.** Pitching HNN/LNN or liquid networks for this physics would draw immediate reviewer objections (conservative/reversible priors on an irreversible system; generic RNN with no physics). Pitching DeepOKAN/PIKAN contradicts the project's own matched-parameter measurement.
- **R8 Search coverage.** Several publisher pages returned 403/405; Crossref metadata stands in for four papers (S15, S22, S23, S24, S29). Their content claims are SEEN-ONLY and should be verified by the project lead before being cited.

## Recommendation in one paragraph

If the project wants a "structure-preserving" contribution that is both honest and new, the defensible sequence is: (1) make the constitutive layer thermodynamically consistent by construction — a learned potential giving q*(c,T) and dH_ads by autodiff (C1-C3), sign-correct LDF with k >= 0 (C5) — inside the existing hybrid/solver pipeline; (2) if appetite remains, build a port-metriplectic / GENERIC latent model of the column with E and S fixed from known thermodynamics and the inlet/outlet/wall as ports, and test what it buys on multi-cycle rollouts. Leave HNN/LNN out; treat LTC/CfC as at most one baseline among neural ODE/CDE for a cycle-level control model; do not promote DeepOKAN/PIKAN.
